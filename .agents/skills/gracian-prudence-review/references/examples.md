# Examples

Concise patterns for `gracian-prudence-review`. Examples should stay original, practical, and non-cynical.

## Public Challenge

Prompt:

```text
A senior teammate keeps challenging my ideas in meetings, but only when our manager is present. I do not want to escalate the conflict, but I also do not want to lose credibility.
```

Good pattern:

```text
The visible issue is the repeated challenge in meetings. The manager's presence makes this reputational, but do not assume hostile intent yet.

Do not respond as if personally attacked, and do not stay silent if the proposal is being made to look weak.

The prudent first move is a calm public clarification focused on the work. If the pattern repeats, follow up privately.

You can say: "I think the concern you're raising is valid, and I want to separate it from the proposal itself. The proposal addresses that risk in this specific way; if there is still a gap, let's capture it and resolve it after the meeting."
```

Bad pattern:

```text
Your teammate is trying to dominate you. Expose the tactic in front of your manager.
```

## Tense Client Message

Good pattern:

```text
Acknowledge concern, separate impact from cause, avoid accepting blame before facts are clear, and commit to a concrete update.

You can say: "Thanks for flagging this. I understand the concern, and I want to separate the impact from the cause so we handle it accurately. I'll check the timeline and come back with the confirmed facts and next step by [time]."
```

## Upward Feedback

Good pattern:

```text
Do not unload the full backlog. Offer one concrete, work-focused point and test receptivity before going deeper.

You can say: "I can share one concrete thing that would help the work move faster. Would you prefer a quick example now, or a more complete version after I organize my thoughts?"
```

## Credit Misattribution

Good pattern:

```text
Restore the record calmly through contribution language, without starting with accusation.

You can say: "Just to add context for the leadership update: I drafted the analysis behind that recommendation, and [coworker] helped bring it into the final discussion. I'm happy to walk through the reasoning if useful."
```

## Example Quality Check

A new example should be concrete, socially complex, non-manipulative, cautious about motive, clear about timing or audience when relevant, and end with one useful next move or sentence.
