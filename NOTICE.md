This project is inspired by broad public-domain themes commonly associated with Baltasar Gracián’s practical tradition, including prudence, discretion, timing, self-command, reputation, social perception, restraint, and practical judgment.

The project applies those themes as an original operational lens for modern socially complex professional situations.
